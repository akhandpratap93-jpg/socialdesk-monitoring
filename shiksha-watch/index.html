Important search note

Google may ignore parts of long Boolean queries and may not index every social post. Run the category searches regularly and verify each original post/article before reporting.

Do not exclude by state name

IIT, NIT, Central Universities, KVS, NVS or other central bodies can be located in any state. Exclude state-government departments and state boards—not the state name itself.

Category-wise keyword library

These bilingual keyword groups are designed for discovery. The author is unrestricted: anyone discussing a tracked central education subject may appear. Combine institution/subject anchors with issue terms; verify the central Ministry connection before marking Direct.

Direct relevance — include

  • Union Ministry of Education, DoSEL and Department of Higher Education.
  • UGC, AICTE, NTA, NCERT/CIET, CBSE, NIOS, NCTE, KVS and NVS.
  • IITs, NITs, IIITs, IISERs, IISc, IIMs and Central Universities.
  • Central exams and processes: NEET-UG, JEE, CUET, UGC-NET and central recruitment/admissions when the responsible body is in scope.
  • Central policies, schemes, funding, official decisions, Parliament/court matters and ministry-linked student issues.

Exclude — out of scope

  • State Education Departments, State Education Ministers and state-government policy announcements.
  • State Board exams/results and routine state university/college matters with no central Ministry connection.
  • Foreign-country education news and generic global education stories.
  • Health-only exams such as NEET-PG, unless a specific central MoE body is materially involved. Foreign education reports and generic global rankings without India/MoE relevance.
  • Posts mentioning a minister's name alone without an actual Education Ministry connection. Any author may be tracked, but the post itself must concern a central MoE body, exam, scheme, policy or institution.
Reporting rule: Search match is not proof of relevance. The author does not matter; the subject does. Confirm the institution, responsible authority, central Ministry connection, location/context, date and original source. Mark uncertain items “Review Needed” rather than Direct. PM POSHAN / Mid-Day Meal incidents are Direct only when linked to the central scheme, guidelines, funding or Ministry; otherwise review as state-level context. ASER, AISHE, UDISE+, NAS, PARAKH, CAG and parliamentary reports should be checked for the India-specific finding and its relevance to Union MoE.
