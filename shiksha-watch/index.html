Coverage safeguards

Important note

This is a search-link builder, not an automatic scraper. X and Google may return incomplete or approximate results. Open the original post/article and verify context, date and source.

Category-wise keyword library

Negative / issue terms have priority. Positive and neutral terms are also available. All categories are flat; there are no nested subcategories.

Include — direct central relevance

  • Ministry of Education, DoSEL and Department of Higher Education.
  • UGC, AICTE, NTA, NCERT/CIET, CBSE, NIOS, NCTE, KVS and NVS.
  • IITs, NITs, IIITs, IISERs, IISc, IIMs and Central Universities, regardless of state location.
  • NEET-UG, JEE, CUET, UGC-NET and other exams managed by central bodies.
  • PM POSHAN / Mid-Day Meal, Samagra Shiksha and other Union education schemes.
  • India-specific ASER, AISHE, UDISE+, NAS, PARAKH, CAG and Parliamentary findings relevant to Union education.

Exclude — unless direct Union link

  • Routine State Education Department announcements and state policy matters.
  • State Board exams/results and routine state university matters with no central connection.
  • Foreign-country education news and generic global education stories.
  • Generic minister-name mentions without an actual education connection.
Classification examples: IIT student suicide → Central Institutions; NTA paper leak → Exams; Mid-Day Meal food contamination → School Schemes; UGC/AICTE corruption → Higher Education; serious CAG education finding → Reports. A story should be assigned to its primary responsible body/topic, not copied into multiple categories. PM POSHAN incidents need a clear scheme connection; otherwise mark for review.
